# RL10A-3-3A validation against test data

FNT-VAL-ANA-002 · Rev A · Released · 2026-11-02 · Owner: Luis Erpenbach

## Question

How well does a fluidnet model of the Pratt & Whitney RL10A-3-3A, built from
published component data and calibrated at one ground-test point, predict the
measured pressures, flows, temperature and speed at the other test points, and
the start transient? How does it compare with NASA's ROCETS model of the same
engine?

## Model and inputs

`examples/rl10a33a.yaml` (steady state) and `examples/rl10a33a_start.yaml`
(start transient) model the RL10A-3-3A closed expander cycle: two-stage fuel
pump and geared LOX pump on one turbopump shaft, venturi, regeneratively cooled
chamber and nozzle jacket (down-pass and up-pass sections), turbine with the
turbine bypass (thrust control valve, TCV), the oxidizer control valve (OCV)
that sets the mixture ratio, fuel and LOX injectors and the thrust chamber.

Sources:

| Data | Source |
|---|---|
| Geometry, component data, typical operating point (tables 2.2.1–2.6.1) | Binder, Tomsik, Veres, NASA TM-107318 (1997) |
| Pump head, efficiency and efficiency speed-correction maps (figures 4.1.1–4.1.8); turbine flow-area map (4.1.16) and efficiency polynomial (4.1.17) | TM-107318, digitized: [FNT-VAL-ANA-003](rl10-maps.md) |
| Test points TP1–TP5 (inputs, ten measured parameters, ROCETS percent errors) | Binder, NASA CR-190786 (1993), tables 1–5; transcription in `validation/rl10/test_data.json` |
| Start transients (time from start signal to 200 psia, nine starts) | NASA CR-195478, table 2 |

Published data that had to be resolved:

- The 2.47 in and 5.13 in chamber dimensions are radii (the 19.19 in² throat
  area gives a 4.94 in diameter).
- The printed 1.05 in² venturi throat would choke at the TP1 fuel flow;
  1.5 in² is used.
- The published design point's pump heads are about 5 % above those of the
  tested pumps.
- The TCV area of table 2.6.1 (0.0158 in², 0.0839 lb/s) is a value "at nominal
  full-thrust condition (predicted by model)", a part-open position, not the
  full-open area.
- Several test values are garbled in the OCR text of CR-190786; how each was
  resolved is recorded in the `notes` field of `test_data.json`.

## Method

### Calibration at TP1

Eight model factors and the OCV opening are solved together with a
least-squares solver (`scipy.optimize.least_squares`) so that the model
reproduces at TP1: O/F and thrust, turbine inlet temperature, the pressure
drops pump discharge → venturi inlet → fuel manifold → chamber and LOX plenum →
chamber, LOX pump speed and chamber pressure. The TCV is held half open at TP1;
its position was not recorded (see the TCV assumption below).

| Factor | Calibrated value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `htc_factor` | 0.937 | Multiplier on the Bartz hot-gas coefficient |
| `jacket_K` | 8.50 | Loss coefficient of the jacket throat region |
| `turbine_area` | 7.462 cm² (1.157 in²) | Turbine nozzle effective area at the area-map reference point |
| `fuel_inj_cd` | 1.030 | Fuel injector discharge coefficient factor |
| `lox_inj_cd` | 0.742 | LOX injector discharge coefficient |
| `head_factor` | 0.948 | Fuel pump head scale (tested pumps against the published design point) |
| `turbine_eff` | 0.788 | Turbine peak total-to-static efficiency (P&W map: 0.744) |
| `cf_eff` | 0.932 | Nozzle thrust efficiency |

All ten residuals at TP1 close to below 10⁻¹² %; TP1 is therefore not part of
the prediction statistics.

### Two checks per test point

- **Free running.** With the factors frozen, only the OCV and TCV openings are
  solved per point to match the measured thrust and O/F, as was done for
  ROCETS in CR-190786. Shaft speed follows from the turbopump power balance.
  The ten measured parameters are compared.
- **At measured speed.** Shaft speed is prescribed at the measured value, the
  OCV is set to the measured O/F and the TCV stays at its TP1 position. The
  other nine parameters, thrust and the turbopump power balance are outputs.
  The power balance is also evaluated with the measured pressures, turbine
  inlet temperature and flows in place of the predicted ones, which tests the
  turbomachinery maps directly.

### TCV assumption

The full-open TCV area is not published. The model valve has 0.16 in²
geometric area (cd 0.9, 0.144 in² effective) and is half open at TP1. This
gives 5.9 % turbine bypass at TP1 and 4.8 % at the typical point, about four
times the 1.4 % that P&W's model predicted for the nominal point. The result
depends on this choice: the TP1 bypass sets the calibrated turbine efficiency
(0.788 at 5.9 % bypass, 0.751 at 1.2 %), and enough bypass to close at TP4 is
what lets the model reach the test thrust there. A larger full-open area at
the same TP1 bypass changes nothing.

"TP1 offset removed" subtracts each parameter's TP1 error from its errors at
TP2–5, which measures how well the trend between points is predicted
independently of the calibration quality. ROCETS was not calibrated at TP1 to
the same extent, so its offset-removed error is the more comparable figure for
trend prediction.

### Start transient

`examples/rl10a33a_start.yaml` uses the same engine with pressure-actuated
valves: the OCV senses the LOX pump pressure rise (100–110 psi), the fuel
cooldown valve FCV1 the fuel pump discharge pressure (250–270 psi) and the TCV
the chamber pressure (442.35–502.35 psi). Ignition is at 0.3 s.

| Case | Time from start signal to 200 psia |
|---|---|
| Measured, nine starts (CR-195478 table 2) | 1.43–2.56 s, mean 1.93 s |
| fluidnet 0.5 | 1.14 s |
| fluidnet 0.5 with P&W peak turbine efficiency 0.744 | 1.29 s |
| fluidnet 0.5 with turbine efficiency 0.70 | 1.57 s |
| Unpublished start-valve settings varied | 0.99–1.39 s |
| Spark time varied, 0.1–0.4 s | 1.40–1.05 s |
| fluidnet 0.4 | 1.51 s |

The start ends within 0.1 % of the TP1 chamber pressure.

## Discussion

1. The free-running error of fluidnet 0.4 at TP3 and TP4 came from the TCV
   assumption: with the published nominal area taken as full open, the valve
   saturates closed and the engine cannot reach the test thrust. With a valve
   that has the range to close (5.9 % bypass at TP1), the free-running
   predictions are within 2.1 % at all points (mean 0.62 %), against 3.1 % and
   1.26 % for ROCETS.
2. The P&W maps are consistent with the test data: with the measured
   pressures and temperatures the turbopump power balance closes within 2.1 %
   at TP4, against 5.3 % with the generic maps. They remove the largest single
   error of the generic model (LOX pump discharge pressure, 3.9 % → 1.25 %) and
   the largest measured-speed error (3.6 % → 2.2 %). On the mean error the maps
   change little, because the TCV absorbs a power-balance error when it is
   solved per point.
3. With the TCV held at its TP1 position, the predicted turbine falls 6–10 %
   short of the pumps at TP3 and TP4. Most of this is the predicted turbine
   pressure ratio (1.381 against 1.424 implied by the test pressures at TP4):
   the measured pressure drop venturi inlet → fuel manifold rises 19 % from TP1
   to TP4 and the model's rises 11 %. Closing the TCV, as the solved openings
   do (5.9 % → 2.0 % bypass), raises the turbine flow and pressure ratio by
   this amount.
4. The turbine inlet temperature, which ROCETS under-predicts by 2–3 %, is
   within 0.84 % at all points. This follows from the enthalpy-based hot-gas
   driving potential ([FNT-HTX-DOC-001](../02-engineering/heat-transfer.md)),
   which reduces the heat flux on the fuel-rich side.
5. The start is faster than all nine measured starts. The P&W turbine curve
   gives 26 % more stall torque per unit efficiency than the generic parabola
   (33 % for the polynomial itself; the table's first segment is flatter), and
   the higher calibrated turbine efficiency adds to it. The efficiency speed
   correction (+0.20 s) and the turbine flow-area map (+0.24 s) slow the start.
   Below 20 % speed the pumps still use the generic zero-speed curves; ROCETS
   used the extended low-speed maps of TM-107318 figures 4.1.10–4.1.15.

## Conclusions

- Steady-state prediction of the RL10A-3-3A at four test points away from the
  calibration point: mean 0.62 %, largest 2.05 % over 40 values, against
  1.26 % and 3.13 % for ROCETS. This rests on an assumed TCV position that the
  test data do not record.
- Open point: with 5.9 % bypass at TP1 the calibrated turbine peak efficiency
  is 0.788, 6 % above the P&W map (0.744); with 1.2 % bypass it is 0.751. The
  power level at TP1 and the trend from TP1 to TP4 are not both consistent with
  the P&W turbine map at the published pump efficiencies. TM-107318 notes that
  its own two turbine flow models disagree at the design point.
- The start transient is 0.3 s faster than the fastest measured start. The
  cargo test bound was widened to 1.0–2.56 s to admit this result; it no longer
  checks against the measured range. Low-speed pump maps are the next step
  ([FNT-PLT-PLN-001](../01-project/platform-plan.md)).
